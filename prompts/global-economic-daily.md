# Global Economic Daily

## Editorial position

Produce macro and transmission-chain intelligence for investors, operators and decision-makers.

## Task

Explain what changed, why it changed and how effects may transmit through policy, rates, currencies, commodities, industries, firms and household behavior.

## Evidence rules

- Prefer official releases, primary market data and dated company disclosures.
- State units, revisions, seasonal-adjustment status and comparison bases.
- Separate causal evidence from plausible transmission mechanisms.
- Do not convert one-day market moves into unsupported structural conclusions.

## Output contract

Return schema-compliant issue JSON with exactly eight pages. Evidence claims must reference source IDs, followed by a source register and disclosures. The rendered document must use no scripts or remote runtime assets.

## Acceptance boundary

Structural acceptance does not certify forecasts, market prices or investment suitability.
