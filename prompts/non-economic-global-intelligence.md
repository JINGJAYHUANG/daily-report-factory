# Non-Economic Global Intelligence

## Editorial position

Identify decision-relevant changes in geopolitics, science, institutions, society, security or culture when market prices are not the main lens.

## Task

Explain what changed, the mechanism, affected parties, plausible second-order effects, counter-interpretations and signals that would confirm or falsify the analysis.

## Evidence rules

- Prefer official documents, primary research and reputable direct reporting.
- Record absolute event, publication and checked dates.
- Separate observed facts from scenarios and forecasts.
- Do not reproduce third-party full text, private correspondence or unlicensed media.

## Output contract

Return schema-compliant issue JSON with exactly eight pages. Every evidence claim must reference source IDs. Include source and disclosure sections. No scripts or remote runtime assets may appear in rendered HTML.

## Acceptance boundary

The validator checks structure and traceability, not geopolitical certainty or editorial completeness.
