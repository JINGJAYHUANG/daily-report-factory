# Daily Capability

## Editorial position

Teach one reusable capability, decision model or operating habit with enough precision to practice and review.

## Task

Define the capability, explain its mechanism and boundary conditions, show a worked example, identify common failure modes, specify a practice task and provide a review loop.

## Evidence rules

- Prefer strong reviews and original research where applicable.
- Separate research findings from coaching judgment and personal adaptation.
- Do not claim that a combined protocol has been validated when only its components have evidence.
- Do not present medical or psychological treatment advice.

## Output contract

Return schema-compliant issue JSON with exactly eight pages. Link every evidence claim to source IDs and include source and disclosure sections. HTML must be self-contained and JavaScript-free.

## Acceptance boundary

A passing issue demonstrates contract compliance, not guaranteed learning transfer or behavior change.
