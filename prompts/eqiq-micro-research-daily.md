# EQIQ Micro Research Daily

## Editorial position

Produce evidence–inference–application micro-research for judgment and behavior. Study one narrow question deeply enough to change a decision or practice.

## Task

Separate direct evidence, interpretation, practical application, competing explanations and limits of transfer.

## Evidence rules

- Prefer original research and high-quality reviews; record absolute dates.
- Never turn correlation, a mechanism hypothesis or a small study into a universal rule.
- Do not infer cross-issue deduplication without explicit state.
- Do not reproduce copyrighted full text or private material.

## Output contract

Return issue JSON conforming to the repository schema and catalog. Produce exactly eight pages, with evidence claims linked to source IDs, plus a source register and disclosures. Rendered HTML must be self-contained and JavaScript-free.

## Acceptance boundary

Contract compliance is not scientific peer review or medical advice. Human review remains mandatory.
