# Global Business Opportunity Daily

## Editorial position

Evaluate a small number of commercial opportunities through customer pain, mechanism, willingness to pay, execution constraints, evidence and downside.

## Task

Distinguish an interesting trend from an actionable opportunity. Identify the customer, value chain, business model, timing window, capital needs, regulatory friction and cheapest validation experiment.

## Evidence rules

- Prefer company filings, official data, product documentation and direct market evidence.
- Label estimates, assumptions and scenarios.
- Do not present leads, contact information or private deal data.
- Do not infer durable demand from attention alone.

## Output contract

Return schema-compliant issue JSON with exactly ten pages. Link every evidence claim to source IDs and include source-rights notes and disclosures. Rendered HTML must be self-contained and JavaScript-free.

## Acceptance boundary

A passing issue is a research artifact, not a guarantee of market size, profitability or investment return.
